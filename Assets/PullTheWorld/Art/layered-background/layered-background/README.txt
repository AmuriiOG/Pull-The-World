Cloud and mountain PNG assets

12 cloud clusters and 14 mountain ranges, each saved as an individual RGBA PNG.
The clouds folder includes the two fine wisps near the sun.
The mountains folder treats continuous ridges as separate assets.

Open preview.html to browse the pieces on light or dark backgrounds.
Open or place the PNGs in Photoshop as individual layers, then position and scale as needed.
Each file has its own canvas size. These assets are not pre-aligned to the original portrait.
reference.png is the original clean background for placement reference.

The source was flattened. Hidden portions and edges outside the original frame were reconstructed with the built-in image generator. Shapes and colors may differ from the source, and the set will not reproduce it pixel-for-pixel.
The generator's soft alpha edges are retained; fine edge cleanup may be useful against strongly contrasting backgrounds.

generation-notes.txt records the generation prompts.

PULL THE WORLD - LEVEL 3 "Tip It Over" - rotated / solved state
This image exists to SELL THE ROTATION MECHANIC. Same level as L3_start, but the whole
world has been twisted around the feet of the player like a wheel.
Target output: 1024 x 1536 portrait PNG.

SCENE
The same two-cube-wide corridor of grass-capped stone cubes, the same stone cradle of
three grey cubes, the same archway door on its plinth at the far end - but the ENTIRE
diorama is rolled about 35 degrees clockwise in the plane of the screen, pivoting
exactly on the feet of the player. Every block, the corridor, the cradle and the door
plinth are all tilted together as one rigid piece; block edges that were vertical now
lean 35 degrees.
The ivory player figure stays PERFECTLY UPRIGHT at the centre of frame on the glowing
cyan anchor disc, and the disc stays flat and level. The player is the one thing in the
image that is not tilted, and that contrast is the entire point of the picture.
The big pale grey faceted boulder has rolled out of the cradle: it is off the low side
of the tilted corridor, mid-air, tumbling away out of the bottom of the frame, with a
small puff of pale grey dust where it left the stone. The corridor it was plugging is
now visibly clear and open all the way to the door.
Gravity still reads straight down the SCREEN, not down the tilted island - so the
falling boulder drops vertically down the frame.
Dressing: the faceted tree from the start state, now leaning over with the tilted world.

STYLE
Stylized low-poly 3D render. Flat-shaded faceted geometry, no textures and no normal
maps at all - every colour comes from flat material albedo only. Clean mobile puzzle
game art direction: soft, uncluttered, instantly readable at phone size, on a mid cool
blue-grey ground.
Muted, restrained, naturalistic colour - premium rather than candy-coloured. Chunky
rounded cubes: every terrain block is a 1x1x1 unit cube with a small ~0.06
chamfer on all edges so edges read soft rather than razor sharp. A grass block is a
stone base with a thin grass cap on top, the cap about 0.18 of the cube tall, and it
overhangs the base by a hair. All materials matte - metallic 0, smoothness ~0.15, no
glossy speculars, no reflections.

CAMERA AND FRAMING
Strict 45 degree isometric. ORTHOGRAPHIC projection - no perspective convergence at
all: every 1x1 cube is the same size on screen regardless of depth, and every vertical
edge is exactly vertical and parallel to every other. Camera euler rotation (36, 45, 0),
locked, never moves. Vertical portrait composition, 2:3. The player figure sits at the
optical centre of the frame. Keep a generous empty background margin all the way around
the diorama - the level is a small floating island of blocks, not a full-bleed
environment.

THE PLAYER
A small chunky faceted humanoid with NO face and no features whatsoever: a rounded ball
head, a soft capsule body, stubby arms, short legs. Off-white ivory. About 1.6 cubes
tall. Always perfectly upright, and always standing on their own glowing cyan anchor
disc - a flat dark disc with a bright cyan glowing ring around its rim, spilling soft
cyan light onto whatever is below it. The player NEVER stands directly on the terrain;
the disc is the fixed point of the entire game.

LIGHTING
Soft single key light from the upper left front, diffuse and gentle, no hard-edged
shadows. Soft ambient occlusion in the crevices where blocks meet. Contact drop-shadows
under the diorama are VERY subtle - soft, wide, and only about 4% darker than the
background plane, never dark pools. Mild bloom on emissive elements only - the cyan
ring, the door glow, fire. Neutral tonemapping, mild vignette darkening the corners.

PALETTE

after bloom, vignette and tonemapping - a render should LAND here.
  background plane   #97A3B3  flat, mid cool blue-grey
  contact shadow     #939EAD  only ~4% darker than the ground - soft, wide, very subtle
  grass cap          shadow #2A562E  base #598146  lit #7B9C55
  stone              shadow #494D52  base #7C8087  lit #A6ABB6
  wood / crate       shadow #6F4D31  base #926540  lit #B2885C
  player ivory       shadow #CDD0DF  base #DBDEE9  lit #EDEEF3
  anchor cyan rim    #86DFFC  the hot emissive core - the BRIGHTEST cyan in the frame
  anchor cyan spill  #5A76A4 -> #7290BB -> #8BB1DB  the wide soft glow around it
  door glow amber    #CEB469 -> #E4C26C -> #F8CE61
  fire orange        #EE6E1E -> #F48429 -> #FB9D1C

SATURATION DISCIPLINE
This palette is deliberately MUTED and naturalistic - noticeably less saturated than a
typical hyper-casual mobile game, and that restraint is most of why it reads as premium.
Do not drift candy-coloured. The grass is a soft olive-leaning green, not a vivid lime.
The stone is a warm-neutral grey, not blue-white. The ground is a mid cool blue-grey,
NOT near-white. Only the three emissives - cyan ring, amber door, orange fire - are
allowed to be genuinely saturated, and the ivory player must stay the brightest
non-emissive thing in the frame.

AVOID
No perspective distortion, no wide-angle lens, no vanishing points. No text, letters,
numbers, captions, watermarks, logos or UI overlay anywhere in the image. No face or
facial features on the character. No photorealism, no PBR metal, no glossy speculars,
no textures, no photographic brick or stone textures, no normal maps. No cluttered
full-bleed environment, no horizon line, no skybox, no clouds, no grass fields. Not
Minecraft-blocky and not voxel art - this is smooth chamfered low-poly. No dramatic rim
lighting, no lens flare, no depth-of-field blur. No visible grid lines or wireframe. The
character must not be tilted or lying down.
No candy-coloured or hyper-saturated palette, no vivid lime green grass, no blue-white
stone, no near-white or white background - the ground is a mid cool blue-grey. No dark
or heavy contact shadows.
